# Meeting Notes 2.6.2

- Teams speaker names and the mute state stay correct while every Teams window is out of sight: minimized, covered by other apps or on another desktop, including the compact meeting view. Teams keeps such windows up to date but reported what they last showed; Meeting Notes now asks Teams for the current state before every reading, without changing anything on screen. A mute pressed meanwhile is no longer missed when Mirror Teams mute is on.
- Someone sharing their screen no longer counts as speaking the whole time: Teams keeps their tile highlighted while they present, so they are named only while nobody else is shown speaking. The shared screen no longer appears as a participant.
- Voices heard only briefly, such as students answering in a lecture, are kept apart from the main speaker, and a short turn that only someone else was shown speaking for no longer takes the main speaker's name.
- Long transcripts scroll smoothly, and the reading position stays put when scrolling, resizing the window or collapsing the header.
- The space bar plays and pauses the recording in the Library instead of Command-P. It still types in text fields and while editing.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
