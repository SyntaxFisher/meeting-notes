# Meeting Notes 2.2.2

- Teams recordings now continue while choosing or sharing a screen, opening menus or chat, resizing the meeting, or minimizing the meeting and its compact view. These interface changes could previously split a meeting into separate recordings and leave gaps in the audio.
- Microphone mute is preserved when Teams temporarily hides its controls. Recording also stops correctly on Teams' post-call screen, without starting an unwanted replacement recording while the capture tracks close.
- Added a Sounds switch in General settings to turn all app sounds on or off.
- Double-click meeting titles and descriptions to edit them in place. Changes save when clicking outside, with Undo and Redo support. Meetings without a description show Add description...
- Enabled agent preferences now add explicit follow-up requests to transcript responses for missing titles, missing descriptions and confident transcription corrections.
- Improved the Copy Transcript button's checkmark feedback.

These fixes apply to new recordings; existing recordings and transcripts are unchanged. Uncertain speaker matches still keep anonymous labels.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
