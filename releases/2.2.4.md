# Meeting Notes 2.2.4

- Outgoing Teams calls start recording when the call connects, not while it is still ringing.
- In 1:1 Teams calls, the other person is named from their tile in the call window, including while the compact call view is open.
- A recording ends when the Teams call window stays open but empty after the call.
- The ages under Last Meetings in the menu bar panel are computed against the current time each time the panel opens.
- Install and Relaunch during a recording or transcription asks whether to stop, save and relaunch; declining keeps the update pending until the app is idle. The quit confirmation has Cancel on the left and Quit on the right.

These changes apply to new recordings; completed recordings and transcripts are unchanged. Speaker names remain estimates and can be corrected.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
