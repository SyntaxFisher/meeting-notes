# Meeting Notes 1.0.2

- Add **Stop & Discard** to stop a recording and delete it immediately without transcription or confirmation.
- Keep auto-record paused after **Stop & Transcribe** or **Stop & Discard** until you leave Teams and join again. Brief gaps in meeting detection no longer restart recording.
- Install downloaded automatic updates and restart once the app is idle, instead of waiting for you to quit. Recording, transcription, permission setup, and a Teams meeting whose recording you stopped remain protected.

Automatic update checks still run roughly once an hour. Versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 retain their install-on-quit behavior until this update is installed.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
