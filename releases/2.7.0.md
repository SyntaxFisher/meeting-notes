# Meeting Notes 2.7.0

- Automatic recordings stop again when the call ends on Macs where Teams keeps a hidden window that repeats the controls of the call in progress. Since 2.6.2 such a window could keep a recording running until it was stopped by hand, and while Teams' compact meeting view was open it made the mute state unreadable, so muting in Teams reached the recording late.
- When Teams leaves the window of an ended call open without its call controls, whatever it shows there, recording stops a few seconds after Teams has let go of the microphone.
- Last Meetings in the menu-bar panel offer the Library's context menu: Copy Transcript, Export Diagnostics, Move to Trash, Retry and Discard, without opening the Library window.
- Meetings recorded from Teams carry the Teams icon in the panel and the Library, and onboarding shows it at the auto-record switch. The app and the README say that Meeting Notes is built for Microsoft Teams.
- Muting in Teams always mutes the microphone in Teams recordings: the setting, the `mirrorTeamsMute` key in config.json and the command-line option are gone. The menu bar, the panel and the Library show the recording state only.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
