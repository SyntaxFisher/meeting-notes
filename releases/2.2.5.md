# Meeting Notes 2.2.5

- A Teams participant whose video tile shows a state such as "Video reconnecting", "Poor network quality" or "Videoverbindung wird wiederhergestellt" keeps a single speaker name instead of appearing a second time with the state attached. All tile states Teams shows in English and German are recognized.
- Merging or renaming speakers keeps the meeting description unless it mentions a speaker name that the change removes. Transcript corrections still clear it.

Participant recognition applies to new recordings; completed recordings and transcripts are unchanged. Speaker names remain estimates and can be corrected.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
