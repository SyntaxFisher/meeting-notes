# Meeting Notes 2.2.1

- Teams speaker names now also work while the meeting window is behind other apps. Previously, voices stayed anonymous unless Teams was in front or showed its compact view.
- More accurate transcripts when people talk back to back: speech is split where the speaker changes, and each passage is transcribed in its speaker’s language. This fixes German sentences that came out in English or went missing after an English speaker.
- Short or unclear passages use the language the speaker usually speaks.
- Removed stray dashes that sometimes appeared between sentences.
- The log now records unusually slow Teams Accessibility reads, to help diagnose delayed mute detection.

These improvements apply to new recordings; existing transcripts are unchanged.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
