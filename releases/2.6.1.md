# Meeting Notes 2.6.1

- Teams speaker names are more reliable when the meeting window is out of sight. Teams stops updating a window that is minimized, on another desktop or completely covered by other apps, and keeps showing whoever spoke last. Meeting Notes now ignores such a window and uses the compact meeting view instead, and it also notices a meeting window whose call clock has stopped.
- A raised hand no longer counts as speaking. Teams draws the same frame around a tile for both, so a participant with a raised hand is only named when Teams marks them speaking as well.
- When several participants speak at the same time, all of them are kept, and a turn at a handover goes to the person who just started speaking.
- Diagnostics record the visibility of every Teams window and all frames Teams draws per participant.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
