# Meeting Notes 2.2.0

- Teams transcripts can now use actual participant names by matching speech to Teams’ active-speaker indicators. Names work with the meeting minimized or in the background when Teams’ compact view remains available. Uncertain matches keep anonymous speaker labels.
- Improved German participant-name detection and local-speaker recognition.
- Fixed mute detection when the main Teams window shows stale controls while the compact view reflects the current call.
- Improved echo filtering in meetings with long muted periods, reducing remote speech incorrectly attributed to you.
- Copy Transcript briefly shows a checkmark after copying.

Speaker identification remains an estimate, especially during overlapping speech. Fully hiding Teams can stop its speaker indicators from updating. These improvements apply to new recordings; existing transcripts are unchanged.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
