# Meeting Notes 2.2.3

- Teams speaker names use FluidAudio's voice evidence from the same recording. Turns that Teams indicators left anonymous take a name when they sound clearly like a participant Teams showed elsewhere, and a voice Teams never showed can be recognized when Teams listed all participants. Every name the previous version chose stays, unless the voice clearly contradicts it: a turn with at least six seconds of its own speech that matches another participant's voice from two independent references, sounds unlike the shown participant, and contains no other voice. Uncertain cases keep their Speaker labels.
- Without headphones, microphone words that repeat what the Teams track said at the same time are removed, so remote speech no longer appears a second time under your name. Short utterances such as a greeting said together with the speaker stay. Recordings with headphones are unchanged.
- While audio is kept, recent meetings keep reconstruction data in the app's Caches folder: the capture tracks, a trace of what Meeting Notes read from Teams and decided, without chat messages or notifications, and the evidence behind each speaker name. `MeetingNotes replay` runs the current app over it for troubleshooting. Turning off Keep audio recordings deletes it, the audio limit and deleting a meeting remove it, and correcting a transcript removes its archived transcription.
- Includes the recording-continuity fixes and Sounds switch from 2.2.2.

These changes apply to new transcriptions; completed recordings and transcripts are unchanged. Speaker names remain estimates and can be corrected.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
