# Meeting Notes 2.2.3

- Teams speaker names now use clear evidence from the entire recording. A later reliable indication can name earlier speech, even when Teams supplied no speaker indicator for most of that voice's speaking time.
- FluidAudio voice embeddings match separately detected voice groups against directly named voices from the same recording. Conflicting speaker groups are resolved per turn, and uncertain acoustic matches remain anonymous.
- Duplicate or simultaneous Teams indicators cannot multiply naming evidence. Inferred names never train further voice matches, and temporary voice embeddings are discarded after naming.
- Includes the recording-continuity fixes and Sounds switch from 2.2.2.

These changes apply to new transcriptions; completed recordings and transcripts are unchanged. Speaker names remain estimates and can be corrected.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
