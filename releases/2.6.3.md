# Meeting Notes 2.6.3

- Automatic recordings in German Teams end with the call again. Teams offers "Erneut dem Anruf beitreten" after a call, which Meeting Notes did not recognize, so a recording kept running until the meeting window was closed.
- German Teams: a shared screen ("Von … freigegebener Inhalt") no longer appears as a participant and its presenter is handled like in English Teams, a raised hand ("Erhobene Hände") no longer counts as speaking, and guests keep their role ("Gast der Besprechung") in the participant list, also with a raised hand.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
