# Meeting Notes 2.3.1

- **Export Diagnostics…** creates much smaller zip files. The mixed recording is left out when the captured tracks are included, since a replay mixes them again. Turning off **Include audio** in the save dialog leaves out all audio, so the zip stays small enough to attach anywhere; `meeting-notes export-diagnostics <id> <out.zip> --no-audio` does the same. The manifest lists what was left out.
- Teams guests keep their name in the transcript when Teams changes their badge after they join from the lobby. Speech that used to split between an unverified and an unfamiliar external speaker now goes to the guest's name.
- A meeting is no longer titled after the Teams join screen. The app waits for the meeting's real title.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
