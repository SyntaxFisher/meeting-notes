# Meeting Notes 2.4.0

- **Copy Transcript** copies the whole meeting as text for pasting elsewhere, such as a chat with an agent on another Mac. Before the turns it adds the title, the recording time, the saved summary, what each speaker name means and the Teams participants with role and the time they were seen.
- Agents reading a meeting through MCP get the same explanations on its first page: whether a name comes from this Mac's microphone, from Teams showing that person speaking, from being the only other participant or from a name given after transcription, and whether Teams showed every participant or only visible video tiles.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
