# Meeting Notes

Diagnostic logs are saved in `~/Library/Logs/Meeting Notes/meeting-notes.log`, with one rotated `meeting-notes.previous.log` (about 4 MB total). Entries include UTC timestamps, lifecycle events, OBS operation results, recording paths and sizes, transcription progress, Teams mute changes, and notification outcomes. Passwords, audio contents, and transcript text are not logged. When reporting a bug, include both log files and the approximate time it happened.

A small macOS menu-bar app that records the Mac microphone and system playback through OBS, then transcribes the saved MP3 locally through FluidVoice. It does not use Codex, Node, or an online transcription service.

Requires macOS 15+, OBS with its authenticated WebSocket server enabled, and FluidVoice 1.6.9+ with its Local API enabled. OBS must have a working `Mic/Aux` input and `macOS Audio Capture` source with macOS recording permission.

Build and install with `make install` into `/Applications`. The app appears only in the menu bar. Click its waveform icon and choose **Start Recording**. OBS launches hidden and stays in the background during recording. When done, choose **Stop & Transcribe**. The icon smoothly pulses red between 75% and full opacity while recording, stays orange while preparing or transcribing, turns red on error, and turns green for 10 seconds when the TXT is ready before returning to idle. Only errors generate notifications. The latest error stays in Notification Center until recording starts successfully or transcription succeeds; routine progress and Teams detection never notify. Use **Copy Last Transcript Path** or **Show in Finder** to find the result. Failed transcription can be retried from the menu.

**Mirror Teams Mute** is on by default and can be turned off in the menu before recording. With macOS Accessibility access, Meeting Notes reads the active Teams call's microphone button: **Unmute mic** mutes OBS's `Mic/Aux`, while **Mute mic** unmutes it. It also works while Teams is in the background. If Teams is closed, Accessibility access is missing, or the button cannot be identified, the OBS mic stays **on** silently. Detection changes and control failures are logged without notifications or icon changes. This means your voice can be recorded even while Teams is muted. **Grant Accessibility…** appears at the top of the menu while access is missing; selecting it requests access and opens the relevant System Settings pane. Access is never prompted automatically. After an ad-hoc-signed update, macOS may require removing the old Meeting Notes entry and adding `/Applications/Meeting Notes.app` again. The installed executable's `--check-teams-mute` argument is a read-only diagnostic.

MP3s are kept in `~/Documents/meeting-notes/audios`; plain TXT transcripts are saved in `~/Documents/meeting-notes/transcripts`. Each matching pair uses its UTC recording-start time, such as `20260923T125842Z`. OBS captures all system playback, not just Teams. Inform participants and follow applicable recording rules.

The app only repairs known OBS recording-format, mute, monitoring, and track settings. Missing devices, capture permission, or the OBS WebSocket server require manual attention. FluidVoice's API returns plain text without speaker labels.
